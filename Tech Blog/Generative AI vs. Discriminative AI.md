### Generative vs. Discriminative AI

| Model Type | Probabilistic Objective | Core Function | Typical Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discriminative** | *P*(*Y* \| *X*) | Predicts label *Y* given input *X* | Logistic Regression, SVMs, Decision Trees, ResNet |
| **Generative** | *P*(*X*, *Y*) or *P*(*X*) | Models joint distribution to sample synthetic data | Naive Bayes, LDA, GANs, VAEs, Diffusion, LLMs |

>  **Key Insight:** Classical ML models like Naive Bayes and Linear Discriminant Analysis (LDA) are formally generative, demonstrating that generative modeling far predates modern deep learning.
