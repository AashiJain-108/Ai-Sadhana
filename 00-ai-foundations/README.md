### 📚 Beginner-Friendly Resource

For building a basic understanding of Artificial Intelligence, I personally recommend the **official CBSE Artificial Intelligence books**.

They are written for school-level learners, so they can be a surprisingly good starting point if you are completely new to AI and want to understand the fundamentals before jumping into Machine Learning, Deep Learning, or LLMs.

You can read the books directly from the **official CBSE website**:

👉 [CBSE Skill Education — Books & Support Material](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/skill-education-books.html)

CBSE also maintains an archive of material from previous academic sessions, which is useful if you prefer an older edition:

👉 [CBSE Skill Education — Books Archive](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/skill-education-books_archive.html)
